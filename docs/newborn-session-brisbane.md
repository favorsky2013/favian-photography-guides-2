How to Prepare for a Newborn Photography Session in Brisbane
A newborn photography session is a beautiful way to preserve the early days of your baby’s life. A little preparation helps the session feel calm, comfortable and enjoyable for the whole family.
Schedule the session early
Many newborn sessions take place during the first few weeks after birth, when babies are often sleepier and naturally curl into newborn poses. It is helpful to enquire during pregnancy and reserve a flexible date around your due date.
Feed your baby before the session
Allow time for a full feed before leaving home or before the session begins. Bring extra formula or expressed milk if needed. Babies may also need additional feeds during the session, which is completely normal.
Pack the essentials
Bring nappies, wipes, spare clothing, feeding supplies and a dummy if your baby uses one. A familiar wrap or small comfort item can also help settle your baby.
Choose comfortable clothing
Neutral, coordinated clothing helps keep attention on your family. Soft colours such as cream, beige, white, brown and muted earth tones photograph beautifully. Avoid large logos and busy patterns.
Keep the day relaxed
Newborn sessions are baby-led. There may be pauses for feeding, settling, changing or cuddling. Allow extra travel time and avoid scheduling another important appointment immediately afterwards.
Prepare older siblings
Explain to siblings that the session will include gentle photos with the baby. Bring a quiet activity or small snack for breaks. Including siblings in simple poses often creates some of the most meaningful images.
Ask questions before the session
Before booking, ask your photographer about the studio location, session length, safety practices, styling, available props, image packages and what happens if your baby is unsettled.
